# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Mac gamers, especially Roblox players, who want quick local replay clips with low overhead while they are actively playing. They are in a focused, performance-sensitive context and need Capturely to stay small, readable, and quiet unless recording or saving state changes.

## Product Purpose

Capturely is a compact native macOS replay recorder. It detects configured game windows, keeps a local replay buffer, and lets the user save recent gameplay manually through a button or hotkey. Success means the user trusts that recording is active, understands what will be saved, and can create montage-ready clips without managing a large dashboard or social feed.

## Brand Personality

Calm, compact, native, gaming-aware.

## Anti-references

Capturely should not look like Medal branding or a social feed. It should avoid oversized dashboards, Valorant-specific styling, noisy animated panels, redundant stats, hover-only critical information, and decorative effects that compete with gameplay.

## Design Principles

1. Keep the recording surface compact enough to feel like a replay console, not a media editor.
2. Show only recording-critical information on the main page; move configuration and storage management to Settings or the sidebar.
3. Use red only for recording state and save feedback.
4. Prefer native macOS structure and familiar controls over custom dashboard chrome.
5. Keep motion subtle, useful, and optional through Reduce Motion.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Respect Reduce Motion. Keep contrast strong on dark surfaces. Do not hide critical status details behind hover. Avoid relying on red alone for state when text or icon labels can clarify the same information.
